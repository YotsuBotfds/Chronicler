# Contextual science: first natural-world execution cohort

This protocol is written before its first natural-world outcome runs. It is
a development execution and exposure check, not a confirmatory benefit study.
The controlled mechanical pilot remains separately documented in
[the scientific-claims contract](scientific-claims-2026-10.md).

## Frozen first run

Use seeds **42 and 43**, ordinary template worlds with **8 regions and 4
civilizations**, **60 completed turns**, hybrid agents and the source's normal
new-world defaults. Explicitly enable scholarship, techniques and scientific
claims; do not seed academies, force action choices, stabilize ecology,
override scientific parameters or enable other new expansion systems. Use
one worker with one-thread numerical/native libraries, a frozen integration
checkout and an identified fresh native binary. Record exact source, package,
runner, registration and binary hashes in the ordinary evidence manifest.

For each seed run a full normal parent to turn 60 while retaining its exact
checkpoint at **completion turn 1**. Restore that same checkpoint for every
arm. The parent continues through the full horizon so its fork prefix does
not gain an artificial final-settlement pass. A trial first funded at source
turn 0 can resolve no earlier than source turn 1, after this boundary. Thus
the chosen boundary precedes initial result receipt without erasing state.
Do not use the harness's general turn-4 default for this cohort.

All four arm configurations are complete and fixed:

| Arm | Withhold information | Transmit | Comparator-centered sham |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `normal` | false | true | false |
| `withheld` | true | true | false |
| `no_transmission` | false | false | false |
| `uninformative` | false | true | true |

Retain all ten actual executions, including both full parents. No-op normal
forks must finish with state bytes identical to their uninterrupted parent.
Other arm metadata must name the shared parent checkpoint and explicit
intervention fork, preserving prior trial/proposal/message state. Controls
do not replace a world's state or retroactively remove old observations.

## Readout and interpretation

Retain every seed, including empty exposures, cancellations, deaths, failed
workers and horizon truncation. Do not select seeds on eventual academy
survival, favorable trial results, observed treatment benefit or receipt
counts. No significance threshold, parameter search, adaptive seed additions
or extended horizon belongs to this cohort. A later exposure-oriented design
must be named and registered separately before its runs.

Report the number and dates of participating academies, contextual proposals,
funded/completed/cancelled/failed trials, actual evidence receipts, pending/
expired messages, trial expenses, paid adoption expenses and allocation
shares. Distinguish pre-fork and post-fork records. Report recorded local
confidence and dependence-block accounting without treating academies,
seasons or climate years as certified independent experimental units.
Test-plot outputs are measurements of existing physical functions, not grain
entering the world's inventories. Empty activity is a finding about exposure.

The withheld arm should produce no new outcome receipts after the fork;
the local-only arm should produce no remote receipts after it; sham trials
retain physical diagnostics/costs while applying the already registered
uninformative measurement rule. Check these channel mechanics and normal
fork identity before interpreting larger world outcomes. Budgets and trial
schedules can diverge under endogenous treasury, eligibility, practice,
movement or survival feedback. Do not label the realized worlds perfectly
spending-matched or claim a fixed patient/trial cohort by filtering after
the intervention.

Example command, after freezing the complete implementation and binary:

```sh
python scripts/run_expansion_experiment.py \
    --source-root /path/to/frozen-integration \
    --feature scientific_claims --cohort development --name science-natural-dev-60 \
    --seeds 42-43 --turns 60 --fork-turn 1 --workers 1 \
    --registration /path/to/frozen-integration/docs/experiments/scientific-claims-development-2026-10.md \
    --output-root /path/to/new/science-evidence
```

The development label remains explicit even with a registration file. A
complete manifest establishes execution under recorded identities; it does
not qualify population benefit, scientific truth, defaults or throughput.
