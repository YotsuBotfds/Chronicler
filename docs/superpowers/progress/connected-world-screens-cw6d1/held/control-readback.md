# CW6D1 supplementary control reading

The frozen preregistration asks for `RC−R0` alongside `RS−R0` to show removal
of the lasting relief label separately from the food service. The generic raw
report's per-arm activation marks `RC` unavailable because this control cannot
purchase food. The table below uses the preregistered **RS service-arm activation**
instead, retaining only worlds 3801 and 3805. This does not change activation,
any recorded outcome or a threshold. With only two activated worlds, there is
no stratum mean, sign summary or sign-flip inference.

| Recorded commons members per 1,000 relief-realm resident-turns | RS−R0 | RC−R0 | RS−RC |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Procedural 3801 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Procedural 3805 | +5.441442 | +0.193050 | +5.248391 |

Short person-turns per relief-region-turn were unchanged between RC and R0
on both worlds; RS−RC was zero on 3801 and −0.440126 on 3805. All other worlds
remain non-activated for this mechanism; no missing denominator is filled.

These are descriptive individual observations, not evidence of efficacy.
Each arm records rates on its own relief realms and turns, which can diverge.
Their arithmetic differences must not be read as a fixed-enrollment causal
decomposition. The one-off grant response and other reform rules remain in all
arms; only the lasting poor-relief credit is withheld by RC/RS.
