## Boundaries and explicit scope decisions

Wealth is the existing single spendable balance, not globally conserved cash.
Ports lack a service consumer. Irrigation is the declared sanitation proxy;
regional epidemic fractions, personal care episodes and mortality remain separate.
Cargo location differs from native carrier residence. Inferred household keys
are not permanent lineages. Bounded samples, dropped rows, unknown income and
unrecorded cause-specific deaths remain unknown; liabilities are not wealth.
A6 classification loss also changes the X1-derived C2 crowding/exposure proxy.

A4-W owned cross-border wares remains an unadopted audit proposal outside the
adopted §5.7 food/timber contract; it is not implemented and is not a technical
port-style deferral. Tax/tithe debtor policy, decay-versus-food policy and
broader relief retain their explicitly recorded later-decision status.

Full 200-seed/procedural qualification and default promotion remain restricted
and unrun. No paid narration, purchase, deployment or credential/sharing change
was performed. Mixed/adverse outcomes and substantial CPU/RSS/artifact/UI costs
support keeping this experimental program opt-in.
