## Result artifact fields after execution

The report should name the final simulation commit separately from later
docs/integration commits and retain source/native identities, source.txt,
the immutable preregistration, metrics hash, raw first-world bundles for
every arm/stratum, Arrow sidecars, exact commands, logs and extraction/schema
failures. Record all 144 expected rows as completed, failed or unrun; do not
replace worlds or condition comparisons on activation.

For both strata retain every world's AD−RD food and epidemic differences,
the four core-arm levels and crowding interaction, component-deletion
contrasts, health-cost and joint food-regime contrasts. Give means, ranges,
signs, descriptive whole-world uncertainty, population/person-turns and
actual activation beside outcomes. Distinguish raw availability shortfall
from measured household-access deficits and their missing coverage. Retain
null ratios, unknown motives, capped input samples and unrecorded famine/war
causes. A native combined-choice receipt does not identify the causal effect
of one score component.

Keep historical CW6 adverse results and their original source/control
limitations visible. Fresh corrected results can reaffirm or change the
observed signs; neither outcome alone proves a software defect, validates a
mechanism or promotes a default. PH cost-only retains the declared crowding
basis and material services; no_relief retains observed free food through
the pre-existing allocation path. Report realized costs rather than claiming
equal spending.

List separately the exact-resume, both-reference feature-off, Python/native/
Rust, viewer/browser, compatibility and same-scale before/after profiling
receipts. The bounded CWR1 screen is not either restricted qualification
gate. Keep scope distinctions explicit: ports and owned cross-border wares
remain absent, and the constructed history example demonstrates visibility
and redaction rather than a separately simulated alternate story.
