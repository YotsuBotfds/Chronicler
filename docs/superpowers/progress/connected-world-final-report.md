## Outcomes and limitations

CW6 used five declared arms in twelve fresh worlds under the unchanged
one-core-hour ceiling. The documented 200-to-120-turn amendment preceded every
held start; actual simulation plus extraction was 0.40761 core-hours. The
200-turn pilot budget rejection and earlier illegal-payment-alias failures
remain recorded. This is bounded screening, not qualification.

FULL minus A6 record-only household balance change per 1,000 person-turns
averaged **−3.41826 template / −1.03271 procedural**. Food shortfall increased
in all twelve worlds (**+1,110.365 / +1,254.741** mean), and recorded epidemic
deaths increased in all twelve (**+22.167 / +151.333**). Native death-rate
contrast against D3 record-only was mixed (**−0.4103 / +0.2822**); the joint
timber/cover service primary was mixed too. These total adaptive contrasts
combine choices, information and X1-derived crowding feedback. They do not
supersede CW1's finding that wages raised credits at fixed enrollment or its
null priced-out effect. No efficacy or default-promotion conclusion follows.

FULL met only 312/684 demanded army rations on template worlds and 1,498/5,465
on procedural worlds. Unknown migration attribution was 85.07%/98.29%; bounded
personal rows and opportunity costs are frequently unavailable. Unknown
migration causes also change X1 classification and its C2 crowding/exposure
input, rather than proving less physical displacement. Cause-specific famine
and war deaths and some forest outcomes remain unrecorded, not zero.

Health's mean death rates were lower against cost-only care (PA−PC), but
procedural raw deaths averaged 2.833 higher against that same control.
Procedural arrivals were 20.833 higher against shuffled reports (PA−PS);
food shortfall increased in both strata against cost-only care. D1 relief activation was sparse and its
primary summary is suppressed; league results were mixed. Invalid historical
relief lower bounds were withdrawn without rewriting raw evidence.

The current history panel initially opens in about 4.8 seconds for the final
120-turn bundle. Substantial simulation/recording costs, adverse physical
outcomes and incomplete personal observations remain material limitations.

Ports remain technically deferred because no maritime service consumer exists.
Owned cross-border wares is an explicitly unadopted audit proposal outside the
adopted food/timber contract; it is unimplemented. Irrigation is a sanitation
proxy. Granary/irrigation-only timber orders can remain unfunded without a
justified work stake. Existing Soldier income remains modeled. Household
groups are inferred, not permanent lineages; restitution liabilities are not
spendable wealth. Tax/tithe debtor and decay-versus-food policies retain their
explicit later-decision status.

Care retains the adopted common `0.3 × coverage` CFR reduction, applied to each
pathogen's distinct base CFR under one combined floor. This resolves the roadmap's
open coefficient choice without inventing differentiated, unvalidated values:
it is **not pathogen-calibrated**, and may overstate care for plague/pestilence.
Per-pathogen deaths and infectious person-turns are already recorded and checked
against regional totals; these outcome splits are not measured causal deaths
averted. Native endemic mortality remains unchanged.

Default-path provenance consumers, merchant origin/current-region income
mismatch and natural deaths counted as army casualties remain recorded rather
than silently corrected. Full 200-seed and procedural qualification gates and
default promotion remain restricted and unrun. The two existing ignored Rust
wall-time benchmarks (6K/24 and 10K/24, 500 turns) were also not invoked; they
remain manual machine-dependent checks, distinct from the completed V11 profiles. No paid narration, purchases,
deployments or credential/sharing changes occurred. No raw workspace lifetime
or automatic restart is promised.
