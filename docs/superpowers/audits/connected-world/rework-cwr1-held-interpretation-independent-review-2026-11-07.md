## Mechanisms, harms and unknowns

All arms retain A6 rules 3 and active D3. Personal record-only has positive D3
inputs; it is not feature-off. Component deletions are marginal deletions from
the complete AD context, rather than additive effects. Deleting migration
information lowers mean food shortfall but also lowers mean person-turns by
3131.166667 / 12035.5 and endpoint population by 36.666667 / 153. Its procedural
epidemic effect is mixed, including a +381-death world. This is not sufficient
to recommend deleting information or call it a welfare gain.

Template no-work primary differences are exact nulls. AD records only two
template offer declines, both in one world; procedural AD records twenty.
Procedural no-work epidemic differences are positive in five worlds and zero
in one. Trade and migration-experience deletions also have mixed or adverse
outcomes. Retained actual/would observations support activation checks, not
attribution of a combined native score change to a particular component.

PH active versus cost-only has exact template primary nulls, and template
RD/AD supplied-care coverage is zero. Observed water service is present, so
this does not establish that every PH input was absent. Procedural supplied
care is sparse (five RD and nine AD region-turns across six worlds), with small
and mixed downstream contrasts. Cost-only retains billing, food/farm time,
guards and quarantine rules; responsive spending and delivery need not match.
The experiment neither calibrates the uniform care coefficient nor proves
clinical benefit or absence of benefit.

The joint no-relief ownership/allocation regime can reduce regional shortfall
while increasing observed priced-out person-turns in every world in both
personal contexts. Mean RNR−RD increases are 1382.5 / 5127.833333; mean ANR−AD
increases are 1390.333333 / 4817.5. It changes titled share and free-pool
allocation jointly; existing free food remains. It is not a pure price effect,
removal of all relief or a welfare improvement.

Priced-out harms are observed provisioning subtotals. The generic harm reader
sums present rows and checks the observed lifetime counter, while skipping
missing observations. Its `recorded` status does not certify universal
household coverage. The stricter CWR extractor separately counts populated
region-turns without provisioning and makes complete access null when any are
missing. All six procedural AD complete access totals are unknown (65–373
missing region-turns); its observed subtotals cannot establish world-wide
affordability. Enrollment is a common eligibility recipe with endogenous
realized coverage. Regional availability and household access remain distinct.

The AD procedural component history is capped in 659 of 720 turns; complete
score merges are independently reconstructible in only 61. All component rows
outside retained samples remain unverified. Work unknowns also remain visible.
Source/native tests and exact resume are separate evidence for installation
correctness; finite receipts do not reveal every individual causal path.
Arrival diagnostics remain T−1 physical observations, not inferred motives or
housing exposure. Cause-specific famine/war deaths are null in all 144 rows,
not zero; epidemic and overlapping native/campaign counts are not added into a
fabricated mortality census. Missing forest harms remain missing too.
