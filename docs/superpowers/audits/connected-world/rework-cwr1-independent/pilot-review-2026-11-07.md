record-only and channel-deletion inputs are inactive as prescribed, active

motive-classified crowding. Realized D3 inputs need not be equal across worlds
whose physical trajectories respond differently. Native combined-choice
receipts do not attribute realized changes to a particular component.

matching the actual pre-existing `no_relief` semantics. The review does not
reintroduce the disproved assumption that this mode removes every free
ration. Complete household-access deficit is legitimately unknown in 12 of 24
runs: for example, AD-p19 has 248 unobserved populated region-turns and AD-t5
has 2. Observed subtotals and coverage remain visible; those missing values
are not favorable zeros or complete access measurements. The regional
availability-shortfall primary remains a distinct observed quantity.
Famine-cause and war-cause deaths remain unrecorded/null in every row.

These checks validate metric receipts and their control implications. They
do not independently reread authoritative per-turn frames, establish clinical
effects, qualify the program or promote defaults. Producer strict extraction,
source/contract tests, raw-bundle review, exact resume, feature-off identities,
browser journeys and declared-scale profiles remain separate gates.
