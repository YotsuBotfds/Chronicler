have zero supplied-care coverage and patient food, while AD still pays 110,328

or mixed: the health epidemic interaction averages −5.666667, with two
negative and four zero worlds. Cost-retaining rules need not equalize
realized spending or trajectories.

The no-relief comparisons remain joint title-fraction/L2-allocation regimes,
not pure price or food-access effects. ANR−AD mean food availability shortfall
is lower (−633.587951/−1386.976392), while **observed** priced-out person-turns
are higher in every world (+1390.333333/+4817.5 on average). RNR−RD priced-out
subtotals are also higher in every world. Coverage is endogenous; these are
observed subtotals, not complete household-access totals or a welfare gain.
