Phase 10 order: `timber_supply` before `timber_orders`.
- Chain: timber_stock (L3, native cap per slot) -> commons_rules (L8, tightens the cap) -> repair_projects (RS2 need) -> timber_supply (PL1 crews, store) -> timber_orders (A4-3 carriers, escrow).
- Sizes: about 12.6k lines across 30 modules in this area.

**Findings**
1. [high] bug — Commons bill can be planned against coins that a later Phase 2 spender uses. `commons_rules.pending_phase2_bills` (`commons_rules.py:226-238`) subtracts only grain storage and workshop bills, not RS2 (`repair_projects.pending_bills`) or timber_supply expedition wages. Commons prepares first in hook order. Settlement raises if the treasury is short (`commons_rules.py:443-446`), and timber_supply's `finish_phase2` raises on the same condition (`timber_supply.py:757-759`). Its `_budgets` (`timber_supply.py:580-589`) omits commons bills. Suggested action: add a tight-treasury test with a commons rule and an expedition in one turn; if it fails, route every Phase 2 bill through one shared pending ledger. Unverified by test.
2. [med] fragile — Switch validation checks saved dependents only when the flag is given explicitly. `livelihoods.py:772-786` and `:765-789` test `values[...] is True`, so resuming with `--no-repair-projects` while `recovery_crews`, `veteran_reintegration` or `maintenance_obligation` are saved on may pass. `timber_supply` (`:794-802`) and `timber_orders` (`:889`) use `_on`, which includes saved state. Suggested action: one generic dependency table checked with `_on`, plus a test per dependency. Unverified whether another check covers these.
3. [med] bug/fragile — Hard-coded transit decay. `timber_orders.py:266` (`0.99 ** hops`), `:327` (via `TT._capacity`) and `:391` (`0.99`) use 0.99 instead of `economy.TRANSIT_DECAY`, which `timber_supply.transit_keep` (`timber_supply.py:167-170`) reads. A change to the economy constant would split the two accounts. Also, `_needs` (`:266`) omits route risk that phase 10 applies (`:391`). Suggested action: use `TS.transit_keep` everywhere.
4. [med] conservation — Timber appears from nothing when a slot becomes timber or the stock is switched on. `timber_stock._opening` (`timber_stock.py:161-171`) and `set_timber_stock_enabled` (`:189-208`) open unheld slots at effective capacity. The ledger identity (`_check_row`, `:356-366`) holds because opening is not a checked flow. Suggested action: record an `initialised` flow that sums to the stock.
5. [med] ordering — Expedition coin check ignores pending bills. `timber_supply._try_launch` (`:1214-1217`) compares the bill to projected treasury without subtracting what RS2 and granaries plan this turn, which `_budgets` subtracts at prepare. The fallback is the unpaid-recall path, so this is a recall, not a crash. Suggested action: use the same budget function at launch.
6. [med] fragile — Implicit cross-package ordering. `timber_orders._needs` (`timber_orders.py:240-271`) reads `timber_supply` `_turn.needs` and the infrastructure timber needs, and works only because `timber_supply.phase10_before_reserves` runs earlier in `LIVELIHOODS_SWITCHES`. Suggested action: pass the needs as an explicit argument.
7. [med] fragile — `timber_orders.set_timber_orders_enabled` (`timber_orders.py:42-52`) checks only enabled flags; the full check lives in `livelihoods.py:889`. Any other caller bypasses it, and `deliver_ordered_store` raises if the stock is off (`timber_supply.py:466-468`). Suggested action: call `timber_orders_active`-style validation inside the setter.
8. [low] dead-code — `timber_supply.pending_bills` (`timber_supply.py:547-560`) has no caller in `src/`, `tests/` or `scripts/` (grep for `pending_bills`, `TS.pending_bills`, `timber_supply.pending_bills`). Its docstring says "nothing reads it yet". Only `docs/integration/timber-supply.md:403,655` mention it.
9. [low] duplication — Three counter helpers and three controller lookups. `_bump` at `timber_supply.py:236` and `timber_orders.py:31` raise on undeclared keys. `commons_rules._bump` (`:404`) does not, and receives keys built from decision reasons (`:546`). Controller lookups: `timber_orders.py:76`, `commons_rules.py:254`, `timber_supply.py:304`.
10. [low] conservation — Deliberate money sinks, recorded. Prepaid wage remainders are written off when a civ loses its regions or the switch is off (`timber_supply.py:283-293, 1145-1146`). Commons wardens' cost is a `keep` treasury charge (`commons_rules.py:504-506`). Escrow residuals return to the destination through `_refund` (`timber_orders.py:96-105`). Each route has a counter; I found no leak in the read modules.
11. [low] unbounded growth — None found in the read modules. Caps: `MAX_CLOSED`, `MAX_HISTORY`, `MAX_DROPS`, `MAX_NEEDS`, `MAX_ORDERS`, `MAX_EVENTS`, `MAX_DECISIONS`. `timber_supply.deposits` is filtered per turn (`:1353`). Keys of `prepaid`, `store` and `prices` are bounded by civs or regions.
12. [low] test-gap — Ablation controls (`enforce_cap=0`, `cost_only`) are real modes: `timber_stock.py:80-86`, `timber_supply` `cost_only` (`:712`), `commons_rules` `cost_only` (`:389-390`). None is dead, but the claim that they keep identical bills depends on tests I did not read.

**Simulation depth**
- Replace the three per-package ledgers (stock, store, orders) with one per-region timber ledger that has explicit transport, decay and seizure flows. That would make conservation a single check.
- Commons rules currently pay wardens and raise grievance only. Link the quota to observed local food stress and to the stock's regrowth water gate (already in `timber_stock`), so rules react to the ecology and not only to a fixed threshold.
- Add an explicit regrowth cost to the native economy so cutting above MSY lowers future labour productivity, instead of only depleting the stock.
- Give expedition crews a labour opportunity cost tied to their farm output during cutting, already partly modelled as `away` labour.
