# Civilization lifetime capacity admission — 2026-10-30

This source correction makes the existing 254-record civilization limit an active lifetime-identity boundary. Civilization indices cross the agent bridge as UInt8; 255 means no civilization, 254 remains unused, and valid civilization records occupy indices 0–253. Dead and absorbed records still reserve their original identities. Increasing the number of regions does not increase this identity budget.

## Creation and restoration behavior

At 254 records, pure political checks decline a new identity and emit at most one `polity_capacity_refused` event per check with the reason `civilization lifetime capacity 254 reached; no new identity allocated`. Accepted earlier secessions remain queued and are committed: the reservation counts the existing registry plus the pending queue. At 253, two eligible secessions can therefore create only the first child in the existing order. A rejected creation does not debit treasury, split statistics, transfer agents or territory, reset regional population, or remove the restoration's exile record.

Restoration of the same existing dead civilization reuses that record and is legal at capacity. A restoration that needs a new record must reserve a slot. This does not recycle another dead identity or compact the registry. The existing four-slot headroom and concession/crackdown fallback in the city and movement controllers are unchanged. Their private creators and the shared transfer helper additionally refuse before their first local mutation or callback.

The native politics planner checks the original input record count plus the number of accepted pending New identities, before emitting a new civilization's operation bundle or updating its live state. Rejected allocation leaves the pending ordinal unchanged. It emits one existing EventTrigger per rejecting step with a known Existing actor; it introduces no result fields, operation families or native payload schema. Existing dead-record restoration does not allocate. The two original allocation sites, all below-capacity arithmetic and random draws, and accepted local-id order are retained.
