# Individual work-offer adapters

A6 rules2 can decline new repair, granary, woodcutting and infrastructure
work offers from dated personal work experience. Existing expedition
commitments retain their schedule; the offer adapter is not called again to
silently end a crew's journey. The planners apply ordinary age, residence,
trip, enlistment and registry exclusions first. New eligible people then ask
`accepts_offer`; no declined person supplies anonymous replacement labor.
The adapter is inert when A6 is absent or disabled. Rules1/record-only offer
behavior remains the A6 kernel's authority.

Each job reports its exact personal accepted wage before any aggregate or
bounded viewer receipt can truncate it. Repair and woodcutting retain the
native transfer's individual applied amounts in their transient turn;
granaries and infrastructure use their named member receipts. The shared
native paid-work receipt supplies withholding only when its total paid share
matches this job's share. A combined receipt that cannot be attributed to a
single job stays unknown. Unknown withheld costs and retail counterfactual
harvest titles never become zeros or regional-average money estimates.
Native ordinary income and wealth remain the existing authorities.

Kinds are `repair` (RS2 workshop repair), `granary` (PL3 granary work),
`woodcut` (PL1 new expedition) and `infrastructure` (B3 irrigation/roads).
Nonfarmer title forgone is zero only when the person's Phase2 occupation is
known; farmer titles inside the active household-retail pilot remain unknown.
The helper adds no native purpose, native occupation choice or second balance.

Contract tests cover combined-share and unknown-title refusal to infer a net,
known personal withholding, disabled identity and an actual named planner's
personal decline with no anonymous fallback. Full A6 integration evidence must
use the integrated source, as the recovered B3 development base predates A6.
