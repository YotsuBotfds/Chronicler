`--grain-reserves --public-grain-shipments` with an agent-backed goods economy
gives foreign public grain a delivery date. Domestic purchasing retains its
existing priced producer payment. Foreign delayed orders require one direct
permitted merchant-graph edge; farther foreign sources are skipped while this
option accepts orders. Nothing changes private merchant cargo title or cash.

An order paid in Phase10 of T leaves its source market immediately, arrives in
Phase10 of T+1, and can first feed people at the existing Phase2 opening of
T+2. This deliberately misses one opening compared with immediate procurement.
Existing deterministic transit decay is charged once at dispatch. There is no
stochastic loss, route interruption after dispatch, insurance, escort or
rerouting. Later route closure does not stop an already-paid order.

The buyer owns detached net cargo until delivery to its named granary. It is
excluded from regional stockpiles, edible/exportable totals and Native loading
reservations; procurement alone counts it as expected inbound when choosing a
remaining target. Payment debits buyer and credits the foreign ruler exactly
once. Delivery has no new payment. It does not create private producer cash.

Arrival credits the destination's recorded granary keeper. Conquest since the
last Phase2 is handled by the next existing keeper settlement before release.
Original payer and current keeper are reported separately; delivery to another
ruler is captured cargo, never a refund or a second sale. Ungoverned deliveries
enter the granary and are abandoned to its local market next Phase2. A dead or
landless buyer retains its historical index and receives no invented estate.
Removed/reordered/replaced region identities refuse active execution before
turn effects, preserving pending cargo for inspection instead of retargeting.

The ledger caps pending orders at64 globally and two per destination. Capacity,
route, identity, finite cargo and available projected cash are checked before
debit. A full cap skips the order without fallback, eviction or ID consumption.
These conservative parameters are uncalibrated; report cap incidence and
allocation bias before increasing them for larger worlds.

`--no-public-grain-shipments` stops new delayed orders, preserves the monotone
ID counter and drains paid cargo. New foreign buys then follow immediate
procurement while inbound commitments still prevent duplicate target buying.
Disabling grain reserves with cargo pending refuses. A saved agents-off world
keeps its cargo dormant; reactivation settles overdue orders once at the next
active Phase10. Missing economy data defers delivery and new procurement.

Evidence IDs derive from both dispatch turn and integer order counter. They
remain distinct after an empty parent granary system is discarded and later
reenabled. This assumes ordinary monotone world turns and the pending-cargo
disable guard; it promises no uniqueness for arbitrary edited time reversal.
Fork provenance remains separate. No World field or global counter is added.

This synchronous contract does not roll back an entire turn, process death,
