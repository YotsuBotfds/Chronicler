# Recorded finite research agendas

The Learning inspector reads the optional, source-dated
`scientific_claims.research_agendas` observation. Omission keeps the existing
single-cohort view and its publication labels. The viewer recognizes schema 1,
`finite-retained-v1`; a present null, unsupported, incomplete, inconsistent or
future-actual payload withholds the scientific observation and totals. An unknown
mode never silently becomes legacy. Final world state supplies no substitute.

The compact agenda view shows research enabled/paused, the open funding cycle,
inclusive original/general funding deadlines, the recorded earliest next opening
and dated blockage. Earliest opening is a plan, not a promised occurrence.
Research pause differs from dormant academy science: already-paid work, messages,
retained knowledge and ordinary eligible adoption may continue. Capacity rows
separate used, reserved and fixed limits; reservations are record space, not
payments. Closed or unfilled opportunities are not fabricated failed trials.

The expandable ledger retains dated opening/closure, stable origin/recipient
seat IDs, canonical topic/paid-trial membership, deadlines and slot reasons.
New cycle recipients are frozen at actual opening. An adopted legacy cycle
explicitly has an unknown historical opening roster and funding deadlines; its
listed seats were observed at activation. New windows are not retroactive. New
or replacement academies do not inherit another institution's receipts, and
renewal does not automatically republish old evidence to a new cohort.

Retained canonical topics include their full registered metric, comparator,
intervention, horizon and context. Current-context belief rows remain separate:
an inapplicable topic can retain archived receipts without a current policy row.
Dependence groups are policy accounting, not certified independent evidence.
Agenda-mode paid allocations show the actual canonical `claim_id` selected when
payment occurred. Later publication/delivery can change the end-turn confidence
without changing that earlier payment or its confidence. Missing paid topic IDs
are accepted only at the exact legacy-adoption source turn, and displayed as
unknown rather than inferred from recipe or current beliefs.

Archive counts describe retained simulation records, not a complete export of
their bodies. `trials_resolved` still contains only that source turn's terminal
rows. A late-start or gapped history cannot reconstruct every earlier resolution
or receipt from counts or cycle membership. Existing spending series retain
unknown gaps and stop at the selected snapshot.

Actual activation, opening, closure, blockage, topic and receipt dates must be
known by source S, displayed only at snapshot S+1. Future funding deadlines and
earliest-opening plans are allowed. The three agenda event kinds (opened, closed,
capacity blocked) use the shared S-to-S+1 clock in feeds, pinned inspectors,
provenance and navigation. Links require an actual timeline event already known
by the selected source; cycle IDs do not fabricate events.

The reader tests import the unchanged producer fixture
`tests/fixtures/scientific_research/source0.json`. It is a controlled mechanical
unit summary (two seeded academies, two running paid trials), not a complete
`execute_run` bundle or world qualification. Empty core snapshot scaffolding is
test-only. Explicit synthetic/adversarial mutations test pauses, adopted legacy
unknowns, payment attribution, unsupported metadata and future actual dates;
they are separate from actual runtime evidence. Production browser QA waits for
a complete exported run and does not load a demonstration as production data.

Focused verification passed 87 tests across seven files with one worker,
including legacy science, transport and mounted shell event navigation. ESLint
and the TypeScript/Vite production build passed. No broad viewer suite, browser
run, simulation cohort or native qualification is claimed by this change.
